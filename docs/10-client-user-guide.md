---

## 2. Browsing & Filtering Menu Items

1. **Category Tabs**: Click on categories like **Milkshakes**, **Fresh Juices**, **Smoothies**, **Burgers**, or **Desserts** to view relevant items.
2. **Search Bar**: Type any product name, fruit ingredient, or tag (e.g., *"Avocado"*, *"Boba"*, *"Mint"*) for instant live results.
3. **Product Badges**: Look for **Popular** star tags for house specialties.

---

## 3. Placing an Order

1. **Add to Cart**: Click the **+** button on any menu card to add it to your shopping bag.
2. **Review Cart**: Click the floating cart icon in the bottom right corner to adjust quantities or select add-ons (such as **Add BOBA +LKR 100**).
3. **Checkout**:
   - Enter your **Name** and **Phone Number**.
   - Select payment method:
     - **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Pay cash when receiving order.
     - **Online Bank Transfer**: View commercial bank details, place order, and click **Send WhatsApp Receipt** to send your payment screenshot to café staff.
4. **Order Confirmation**: An order confirmation screen displays your unique Order Number (e.g. `#JV-1048`) for tracking.
