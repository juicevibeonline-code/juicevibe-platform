## 📌 Instructions for Submission to Register.lk / LK Domain Registry

1. **Print on Company Letterhead (If available):**
   - If printed on company letterhead, stamp with the official Juice Vibe rubber stamp over the signature block.
2. **Attach Copy of National Identity Card (NIC):**
   - Attach a clear photo/scan of both sides of the owner's NIC (or Passport).
3. **Submission Process:**
   - Scan the signed letter + NIC copy into a single PDF document.
   - Upload via the Register.lk portal during domain registration or email to **support@register.lk** / **hostmaster@nic.lk**.
4. **Follow-up:**
   - Once approved by LK Domain Registry, update DNS records according to [domain_deployment_guide.md](file:///d:/Clients/Juice-Vibe-Pro/juicevibe-platform/docs/domain_deployment_guide.md).
