## 1. Incident Severity Definitions & Response Times

During active support periods or under an optional Monthly Maintenance Agreement, technical support requests are triaged according to the following SLA matrix:

| Severity Level | Definition / Example | Initial Response Time | Resolution Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1 - Critical** | System completely down; storefront or admin desk unreachable. | `< 2 hours` | `< 8 hours` |
| **P2 - High** | Major feature impaired (e.g. checkout form failing or DB error). | `< 4 hours` | `< 24 hours` |
| **P3 - Medium** | Non-critical bug (e.g. minor visual alignment or broken link). | `< 12 hours` | `< 48 hours` |
| **P4 - Low** | General technical question or feature enhancement request. | `< 24 hours` | Scheduled release |

---

## 2. Optional Monthly Maintenance Plan (LKR 8,000 / month)

Following the complimentary 30-day warranty period, the client may subscribe to an optional professional maintenance retainer at **LKR 8,000.00 per month**.

Included Services:
- Priority P1 & P2 incident response.
- Dependency security updates (`pnpm update`).
- Database health checks and manual monthly SQL backups.
- Minor content and price list updates (up to 2 hours per month).
